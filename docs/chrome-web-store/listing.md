## Detailed description

Keep your desktop Chrome tabs within reach on your phone.

TabMirror privately syncs your open Chrome windows, tab groups and tabs to your account at tabs.portuit.com. Sign in on another device to browse the latest saved view, search by title, URL or group name, and open individual links.

• Preserve window and group organization, group colors and pinned-tab indicators.
• Search your saved tabs and expand or collapse groups.
• See when the desktop last checked in and when its tabs changed.
• Sync automatically every two minutes, or choose Sync now.
• Pause syncing, disconnect, revoke a device or delete its saved data.
• Incognito windows and unsupported URLs are excluded.

This private release is for approved TabMirror accounts. Installation alone does not grant account access. Pair the extension once in desktop Chrome, then sign in to the same account on your phone; the phone does not need an extension.

Approving pairing uploads eligible open-tab titles, full URLs, window and group details to TabMirror's AWS-hosted service. Desktop Chrome must be running to send updates. The website refreshes separately, so changes can take approximately four minutes to appear. This is a saved view, not remote control of your desktop.
