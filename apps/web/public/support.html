Help with TabMirror

A private Chrome extension and website for approved users. Contact daniel.portuit@gmail.com for support or data-deletion requests. Never send passwords, session tokens, pairing verifiers, or sensitive tab URLs.

Connect your desktop

  1. Install the production TabMirror extension in desktop Chrome.
  2. Choose “Pair with my account”, then sign in at Account & devices.
  3. Enter the new code, compare its device name, and approve once. Keep desktop Chrome open until connected.
  4. On your phone, sign in with the same Google account and choose “Your tabs”. You do not need to pair the phone separately.

Pairing errors

Codes expire after ten minutes and can be used only once. Codes from the local development extension do not work on this website. After repeated attempts trigger a rate limit, stop trying for ten minutes, then generate a fresh production code. If a device is already paired, revoke it before pairing a replacement.

Tabs have not updated

Automatic Chrome sync and website polling each run about every two minutes; a change may take about four minutes to appear. A sleeping desktop or paused sync takes longer. Choose “Sync now” in the desktop extension, then “Refresh” on the website.

Remove data

In Account & devices, revoke a connection to stop uploads, or choose “Delete device and data” to remove its saved tabs. Sign-out and uninstall do not delete cloud data. Contact support for account deletion.
