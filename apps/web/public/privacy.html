TabMirror

Privacy policy

Effective October 2, 2026. This policy covers the TabMirror Chrome extension and tabs.portuit.com, a private service for viewing your desktop Chrome tabs on another device.

Who operates TabMirror

TabMirror is operated by Daniel. For privacy questions, access requests, or account deletion, contact daniel.portuit@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Open-tab data: titles and full HTTP/HTTPS URLs (which may include query parameters and fragments), window and tab identifiers and order, pinned state, window focus, group names, colors and collapsed state, capture times, revisions and counts of omitted tabs. URLs and titles can contain sensitive personal information.
  • Account and device data: your Google account identifier and verified email address, your chosen device name, pairing and authentication records, connection state and sync timestamps.
  • Local application data: the extension stores its credential, pairing state, device settings and sync/recovery state in Chrome local storage. The website uses a session cookie and local interface preferences.
  • Operational data: requests include IP addresses and standard network metadata. We use IP-derived rate-limit keys to prevent abuse. Application/API logs record operational status, request identifiers and timing. They are designed to exclude tab titles, URLs, credentials and request bodies.

TabMirror does not read page bodies, form entries, passwords, browser cookies from other sites, or the Chrome browsing-history database. It excludes incognito windows and unsupported URL schemes. It reads open tabs after you connect the extension; periodically captured open tabs are still browsing activity, even though we do not intentionally keep a browsing-history archive.

Why we use it

We use these data only to authenticate approved users, pair their Chrome profiles, sync and display their own latest saved tabs, provide search and freshness information, recover failed syncs, and secure and maintain the service. We do not sell data, use it for advertising, credit decisions, or AI model training, or track visits for unrelated analytics.

When syncing happens

After you approve pairing, automatic syncing runs approximately every two minutes while desktop Chrome can run. Manual sync and connection controls can send updates sooner. The website polls separately, so displayed changes may take approximately four minutes. Pausing stops automatic updates and retains the last saved snapshot.

Storage and sharing

Amazon Web Services processes and stores application data for us, primarily in the US West (Oregon) region. CloudFront delivers the website and API through its global network. Google handles sign-in; TabMirror does not receive your Google password. The current service is restricted to approved accounts. Each account can access its own saved tabs, not another user's tabs.

Data travels over HTTPS and cloud storage uses encryption at rest. This is not end-to-end encryption: the service processes readable data, and authorized operators with infrastructure access can access it when necessary to support or secure the service. We do not routinely inspect private tabs. We may disclose information when required by applicable law or to protect the service and its users. External sites you open from a saved link apply their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

The latest saved snapshot remains until replaced or deleted. Superseded, failed and deleted snapshot objects are queued for cleanup; a job runs every fifteen minutes, but failures can delay physical deletion. We do not intentionally maintain snapshot history or S3 snapshot versioning. Account/device metadata has a rolling seven-day database recovery window. Operational log retention is fourteen days, with asynchronous expiry. Expiring authentication and rate-limit records are also removed asynchronously.

Sessions and device credentials can renew during use, with up to 365 days of inactivity before expiry. Revoking a connection blocks further uploads but retains the last saved tabs. Delete device and data removes the saved device from active access and schedules its snapshot for deletion. Signing out, uninstalling the extension or clearing browser storage does not delete saved cloud data. Email us to request deletion of remaining account records; restricted backup copies expire with the recovery window.

Your choices

You can pause or manually sync in the extension, disconnect it, revoke its connection, delete its device data on the website, or uninstall it. Only pair a Chrome profile whose eligible open tabs you want uploaded. Avoid placing confidential tokens in URLs. You can request access, correction or deletion through the contact above.

Limited use and changes

TabMirror's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We will update this page when data practices change and disclose material changes through the service before collecting data for a materially different purpose.
